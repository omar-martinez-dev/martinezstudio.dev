Studio principles

A small set of standards,
applied all the way through.

01

Attention is finite.

Primary workflows stay calm, direct, and free of interruptions that compete with the user’s goal.

02

Privacy is interface design.

System permissions and business models are explained in product language before a decision is requested.

03

Local is a feature.

When a backend is not essential, on-device storage keeps products fast, available, and easier to trust.

What comes next
