Website privacy

Privacy,without guesswork.

Last updated September 6, 2026

At a glance.

This policy explains what martinezstudio.dev handles when you visit the website or contact Martinez Studio.

In short: This website has no user accounts, purchases, comments, newsletters, first-party analytics, or advertising trackers.

1. Scope

This policy applies to the Martinez Studio website at martinezstudio.dev. Ostinova is covered by its separate app privacy policy.

2. Information we handle

The website does not provide user accounts, purchases, comment forms, or newsletter sign-ups. Martinez Studio does not add first-party analytics or advertising trackers to the website.

  • Technical request data. Like most websites, the server or hosting provider may process an IP address, browser and device details, requested pages, timestamps, and similar logs needed to deliver, maintain, and secure the site.
  • Support communications. If you email support, Martinez Studio receives your email address, message, and anything else you choose to include.

3. How information is used

Technical request data may be used to operate, troubleshoot, and protect the website. Support communications are used to answer your request, investigate issues, and keep an appropriate record of the conversation.

4. Cookies and tracking

Martinez Studio does not use cookies, local storage, session storage, cross-site tracking, or targeted advertising on this website. Because the site does not track visitors across websites, it does not respond differently to browser “Do Not Track” signals.

5. Sharing and external links

Martinez Studio does not sell personal information or share it for targeted advertising. Information may be handled by service providers that host or secure the website and email service, or disclosed when required by law or necessary to protect rights, safety, and the integrity of the service.

The website links to third-party services such as GitHub. Their privacy practices apply after you follow those links.

6. Retention and security

Support communications are kept only as long as reasonably needed to respond, maintain relevant records, and meet legal obligations. Technical logs are retained according to the operational practices of the hosting provider. Reasonable safeguards are used, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

7. Your choices

You may ask to access, correct, or delete personal information Martinez Studio holds about you, subject to applicable law. Send requests to support@martinezstudio.dev. Martinez Studio may need enough information to understand and verify a request.

8. Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when the website or its practices change. The date above identifies the latest version. Questions about this policy can be sent to support@martinezstudio.dev.
