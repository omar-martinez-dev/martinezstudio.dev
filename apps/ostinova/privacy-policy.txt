PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated September 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Martinez Studio ("we," "us," or "our") handles information when you use the Ostinova iPhone app. Ostinova's initial release is distributed only through the United States App Store. It covers information that stays on your device, optional product analytics processed by PostHog, information processed by Apple, and information Google may receive when Ostinova requests an advertisement.

Questions or concerns? Contact us at support@martinezstudio.dev.

SUMMARY OF KEY POINTS

This summary provides key points from the policy. The numbered sections below provide the complete explanation.

Most Ostinova content stays on your device. Recordings, videos, recording titles, practice regimens, custom practice instructions, metronome settings, and similar practice content are not sent to Martinez Studio, Google AdMob, or PostHog by Ostinova.

Google AdMob provides app-open advertisements. Google may receive device, network, advertising, interaction, performance, and diagnostic information when an ad request is allowed. Ostinova asks Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) for the applicable privacy choice before it initializes Google Mobile Ads or requests an ad.

Ostinova offers optional product analytics. Nothing is sent to PostHog while your choice is undecided or set to Do not share analytics. If you choose Share analytics, Ostinova sends only a small, fixed set of app-use events and basic technical context. Session replay, automatic screen or interaction capture, and user identification are not active.

Apple and Google provide separate privacy choices. Google's privacy message controls the advertising consent signals applied to Google. Apple's App Tracking Transparency setting controls whether Ostinova can access Apple's advertising identifier for cross-app tracking. You can review available Google choices inside Ostinova and Apple tracking permission in iOS Settings.

1. SCOPE

In Short: This policy applies to the Ostinova iPhone app. The Martinez Studio website has a separate website privacy policy.

Ostinova does not require an account and Martinez Studio does not operate an Ostinova server that stores your practice library. Some features rely on Apple and Google services, which process limited information as explained below.

This policy does not control an outside website, advertisement, or service you choose to open. The privacy policy of that outside service applies after you leave Ostinova.

2. WHAT STAYS ON YOUR DEVICE?

In Short: Your creative and practice content is stored in Ostinova's app storage on your device, not on a Martinez Studio server.

Practice content. Saved practice regimens, block settings, custom instructions, tempos, time signatures, metronome preferences, scale preferences, and other practice settings remain in the app's local storage.

Recordings. Audio recordings, video recordings, recording titles, dates, durations, and related library information remain in the app's local storage. Ostinova does not upload this content to Martinez Studio, Google AdMob, or PostHog.

Device backup. Depending on your Apple device and backup settings, Apple may include app data in an iCloud or computer backup. Apple controls that backup processing under Apple's privacy terms.

User-directed sharing. If you choose an iOS share, export, or backup destination, the selected recipient or service receives the item you chose to share. Ostinova does not share a recording or practice item without your action.

3. CAMERA, MICROPHONE, AND SYSTEM PERMISSIONS

In Short: Ostinova requests access only when a feature needs it, and you can change system permissions in iOS Settings.

Camera and microphone. These permissions let you create audio or video practice recordings. The media is processed and stored on your device. Permission alone does not send a recording to Martinez Studio, AdMob, or PostHog.

Apple tracking permission. If Google's configured privacy flow presents Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt, your answer controls access to Apple's Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA) and cross-app tracking. Denying permission does not disable Ostinova's features and does not necessarily prevent non-personalized ads.

You can review camera, microphone, and tracking permissions in the iOS Settings app. Apple controls the permission interface and system-level behavior.

4. WHAT MAY GOOGLE ADMOB RECEIVE?

In Short: When Ostinova is allowed to request an ad, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect information needed to deliver, measure, secure, and improve advertising.

Device and network information. This may include an IP address used to estimate general location; device or app-bounded identifiers; a rotating SDK instance identifier; and, only when Apple permits access, the IDFA. Google may also generate or use a publisher first-party identifier under its settings and applicable consent signals.

Advertising and interaction information. This may include ads shown, ad interactions, app launches, taps, and video views used for ad delivery, frequency management, aggregated reporting, advertising measurement, and improving ad performance.

Diagnostics and performance. This may include non-user-related crash logs and user-associated performance information such as launch time, hangs, or energy use. Google states that this information may be used for diagnostics, analytics, advertising, and product improvement.

Ostinova does not add your name, email address, recordings, videos, recording titles, notes, tags, custom practice instructions, metronome choices, or practice content to an AdMob request.

Google processes information under its own privacy terms and may use approved advertising partners to serve an ad. Review Google's Privacy Policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy and Google's iOS data disclosure at https://developers.google.com/admob/ios/privacy/data-disclosure.

5. HOW DO ADVERTISING CHOICES WORK?

In Short: Ostinova checks Google's current consent status before requesting an ad, and Apple's tracking choice separately controls access to the IDFA.

Google UMP. Ostinova requests a consent-status update and displays Google's privacy message when required. The app does not initialize the Google Mobile Ads SDK or request an ad unless UMP reports that ad requests are allowed. UMP may retain a valid choice from an earlier app session. When Google requires an ongoing privacy-options entry point, you can reopen it from Settings, Advertising & Privacy in Ostinova.

Personalized ads. If the applicable Google choices and Apple's permission allow it, Google may use permitted identifiers and activity from other apps or services to select or measure more relevant ads.

Non-personalized or limited ads. These are not selected from past behavior, but Google states that contextual information such as general location may be used. Identifiers or similar technologies may still be used for purposes such as frequency limits, aggregated reporting, security, and fraud prevention where permitted.

Apple ATT. Apple's tracking choice is separate from Google's advertising consent. You can change it in iOS Settings. If Apple does not allow tracking, Ostinova cannot access the IDFA for tracking, even if a Google privacy choice would otherwise allow personalized advertising.

No consent state. If UMP has not produced a state that allows an ad request, Ostinova keeps ads paused. Buying Ostinova's Remove Ads purchase also prevents Ostinova from requesting app-open ads for that entitlement.

6. HOW IS POSTHOG USED?

In Short: Ostinova sends a limited set of product-usage events to PostHog only after you choose Share analytics.

PostHog status. PostHog is not initialized and no product-usage event is sent while your choice is undecided or set to Do not share analytics. After you complete your first guided practice, Ostinova may show a one-time disclosure asking whether you want to share analytics. The completed practice that triggers the disclosure is not queued or sent. Every feature remains available if you decline, and you can change your choice later under Settings, App analytics.

Events and technical context. When analytics is enabled, Ostinova may report that the app was opened, the metronome was started, a guided practice was started or completed, or scale playback was started. Ostinova does not attach custom event properties. PostHog may add basic technical context such as app and SDK version, device model and type, operating-system version, screen dimensions, language, timezone, connection type, an anonymous session identifier, and a random identifier for this app installation.

Data boundaries. Automatic lifecycle capture, automatic screen capture, automatic interaction capture, session replay, surveys, feature flags, error autocapture, and person profiles are disabled. Ostinova does not send names, email addresses, advertising identifiers, recordings, titles, notes, tags, tempos, scale choices, practice content, or user-entered text to PostHog. The random PostHog installation identifier is not connected to an AdMob identifier or Apple's advertising identifier.

Hosting, IP handling, and retention. Ostinova uses PostHog's United States cloud service. A network request necessarily reaches PostHog from an IP address, but this PostHog project is configured to discard client IP data after limited in-transit processing such as bot detection or general geographic enrichment. Under the project's current free plan, PostHog may retain product-analytics events for up to one year. Turning analytics off stops future events; previously collected events remain until they expire or are deleted in response to an applicable, verifiable request. PostHog's privacy information is available at https://posthog.com/privacy.

7. PURCHASES AND SUPPORT COMMUNICATIONS

In Short: Apple processes App Store purchases. We receive only the information needed to recognize the purchase, and we receive contact information only when you choose to contact support.

Purchases. Apple processes payment details and maintains App Store transaction records. Ostinova uses StoreKit transaction and entitlement information to determine whether Remove Ads has been purchased or restored. Martinez Studio does not receive your full payment-card number. Apple's practices are described at https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/.

Support. If you email support, we receive the email address, message, and attachments you choose to provide. Do not send recordings or sensitive practice content unless it is necessary for your request and you are comfortable sharing it. Support information is used to answer the request, troubleshoot, protect the service, and meet legal obligations.

8. WHEN IS INFORMATION SHARED?

In Short: Martinez Studio uses service providers only for the purposes described here and does not sell personal information for money.

Google. Information described in section 4 may be processed by Google and its approved advertising partners when an ad request is made. Personalized advertising can involve cross-context activity and may be considered a sale or sharing under some privacy laws even when no money is paid for the information. Your available controls are described in sections 5 and 10.

Apple. Apple processes purchases, system permissions, App Store services, and any Apple backup you enable under Apple's terms.

PostHog. The optional events and technical context described in section 6 are processed by PostHog only after you enable app analytics.

Service protection. We may disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with law, respond to lawful process, enforce rights, investigate fraud or security issues, or protect users and the public. Information may also transfer as part of a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of the business, subject to this policy and applicable law.

We require service providers that process information for Ostinova to handle it for the disclosed purposes and with protections consistent with their applicable agreements, privacy commitments, and legal obligations.

9. RETENTION AND DELETION

In Short: Local content remains until you delete it or remove the app. Providers control the records they process on their systems.

Local app data. You can delete recordings and saved practice items using Ostinova's controls. Deleting Ostinova removes its local app data from that device. Copies already placed in an Apple or computer backup may remain until you delete or replace that backup.

Advertising choices. Google UMP stores consent information so the current choice can be applied and refreshed. Use Ostinova's Advertising & Privacy screen when Google makes privacy options available, or delete the app to remove its local state. Apple's tracking permission can be changed in iOS Settings.

Google data. Martinez Studio does not keep a separate copy of AdMob's ad-request logs. Google determines retention for information on its systems under its policies and legal obligations. Google explains its retention practices at https://policies.google.com/technologies/retention. We will use provider tools reasonably available to us when an applicable, verified deletion request concerns publisher-controlled advertising data.

PostHog data. Under the project's current free plan, optional product-analytics events may be retained for up to one year. Turning analytics off stops future collection. Because Ostinova does not use names, email addresses, accounts, or PostHog person profiles, we may need the random installation identifier or other limited information to locate data for a verified deletion request.

Support messages. We keep support correspondence only as long as reasonably necessary to respond, document the resolution, protect the service, and satisfy legal obligations, then delete or de-identify it when it is no longer needed.

Apple data. Apple controls App Store transaction history, system-permission records, and Apple backups under its own retention and deletion policies.

10. YOUR U.S. PRIVACY RIGHTS AND REQUESTS

In Short: Depending on the U.S. state where you live, you may have rights to know about, access, correct, delete, or restrict certain processing, and to opt out of certain targeted advertising, sale, or sharing.

Privacy choices are available without contacting us. Enable or disable optional analytics in Ostinova under Settings, App analytics. Review Google's available choices under Settings, Advertising & Privacy. Review Apple's tracking permission in iOS Settings. Google also provides advertising controls and privacy information at https://myadcenter.google.com/ and https://policies.google.com/privacy.

To ask about information controlled by Martinez Studio, request access or deletion, withdraw consent where applicable, or appeal a response where local law provides that right, email support@martinezstudio.dev. We may ask for limited information necessary to verify the request and locate relevant records. Ostinova has no user account, so we may be unable to connect an anonymous provider record to your name or email address unless you provide enough information for the applicable provider process.

We will not discriminate against you for exercising a privacy right. Rights are not absolute, and we may retain or decline to disclose information when permitted or required by law. If applicable law gives you a right to appeal our response, reply to our decision or email the address above and state that you are submitting an appeal.

11. CHILDREN AND SECURITY

In Short: Ostinova is a general-audience music practice app, is not directed to children under 13, and uses reasonable safeguards appropriate to the information it handles.

We do not knowingly ask children under 13 to provide contact information to Martinez Studio. If you believe a child has sent personal information to support, contact us so we can review and delete it where appropriate. Parents and guardians should supervise a child's device, advertising, sharing, and permission choices.

Local app content is protected by Apple's app sandbox and the security controls on your device. Information sent to Apple, Google, or a support provider is transmitted and protected through those providers' systems. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

PostHog processes optional analytics in the United States. Google, Apple, PostHog, and their service providers may process the limited information described in this policy in the United States and other countries under their respective privacy terms and applicable transfer safeguards.
