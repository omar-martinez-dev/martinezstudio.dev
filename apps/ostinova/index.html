The product

One continuous practice loop.

Ostinova brings the tools around deliberate music practice into one local-first experience. A musician can choose a regimen, run it against reliable timing, reference scales, record audio or video, and review the result without moving between unrelated apps.

Core experiences

  • Configurable metronome with meter, swing, quick tempos, and background audio behavior.
  • Reusable multi-block regimens for ramps, gap bars, count-ins, repetitions, and recovery.
  • Scale families with configurable roots, spelling, note degrees, and playback.
  • Local audio and video capture with countdown, quality controls, and review.
  • Transparent advertising privacy tied directly to Apple’s tracking choice.
